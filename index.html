The Athens Culinary & Lifestyle Tour

We tried the city for years: the good, the disappointing and the surprising. This day is what stayed. It skips the tourist trail and keeps to the warm, chic corners.

  1. Eat. Tastings that move between traditional Athenian cooking and the new kitchens, with time for every glass of wine.

  2. Walk. The city centre on foot, through neighbourhoods with their own character, street art and graffiti.

  3. Love. Hosting is what we care about most. You are our guests for the day, and we look after you like it.
